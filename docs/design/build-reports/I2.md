### What I wired
**Proposal store and overlay** (`governance/store_pg.py`)
- Proposals, their transitions and the approval mirror are now in Postgres, and so are the overlay's patches, path owners and cooldowns.
- Every store call joins one current transaction, or opens a short one of its own. Overlay changes are written inside the transaction that caused them.
- The `one_in_flight` index is the in-flight lock. A supersede ends the old proposal and inserts the new one in a single transaction.

**Side effects** (`governance/effects.py`, plus a per-call `before(tx)` on `IntentExecutor.execute`)
- The move into the call state (DRAFT to SUBMITTING, DRAFT or NOTICED to AUTO_APPLYING, APPROVED to APPLYING) commits with the intent row.
- The result commits with the intent's result: PENDING or APPLIED (the config mirror records our body as `ours`), or stale, invalid, forbidden or lapsed on an error.
- After a crash, the reconciler calls the same hooks using its own lookups.
- A request that never reached the simulator goes back to DRAFT. `ProposalLifecycle.recover` then resends it with the same id and body if every precondition still holds; otherwise it aborts it and returns its patches to the overlay.
- New transitions: `not_sent`, `abort`, `not_applied`.

**Loop hooks** (`loop/governance.py`, `GovernanceHooks`, the default in `__main__`)
- **REFRESH:** a supervisor version triggers `Overlay.rebase_on_supervisor_edit`, and older pending or approved proposals are marked STALE, all inside REFRESH's transaction. A decision row and a SUPERSEDED-free `SUPERVISOR_EDIT` digest alert are written.
- **LIFECYCLE:** the policy is re-read from the API every step (changes are audited), then `recover`, `tick`, and auto-release of held patches.
- **DETECT and DECIDE:** the probe learner.
- **GOVERN:** `ReleaseTrain.run`, with a decision row per workflow.
- **VERIFY:** `lifecycle.verify`.
- **Step rule:** `plan_facts` adds the routine slots, the submit minute of urgent work and notice expiries. Pending and approved proposals now come from the real `proposal` table.

**Probe-only learner** (`learning/probe.py`, `CTL_LEARNER=probe` by default)
- T4's `DidHealthModel`: DID states are persisted every step, with probes and spreads at planning points.
- T4's grid plus `HourCurvesModel`, fitted on history only.
- `CTL_LEARNER=none` keeps the no-op loop of M1 and M2; the platform tests now set it.

**Velocity ledger:** derived from applied auto-apply proposals, which store their per-(cohort, dim) movement and apply minute (migration 0013). This also fixes a T2 gap: an auto-apply that waited out a notice was never counted.

**Orchestrator:** a new `LoopHooks.attach(rt)` runs before the reconciler. A `loop_stage` table (migration 0014) marks each finished stage. On restart, an interrupted step is finished from its first unmarked stage.

### Bugs found and fixed (each reproduced end to end, then re-run)
1. **`jsonb` re-orders keys, and that changed the simulated world after a crash.**
   - Seen in the crash cell `submit_approval:1:before_t1`: the resent body listed caller-ID weights in a different order.
   - The simulator draws each dial in the order we send the options, so every later dial changed. One probe's health test decided at D0 18:15 in the clean run and at D1 19:15 in the crashed run.
   - Fix: `proposal.request`, `patch.value` and `patch.evidence` are now `json`, which keeps the exact text (migration 0015).
   - After the fix, the two runs' simulator logs are identical except for decision ids quoted in rationale text.
2. **A restart skipped the rest of the interrupted minute.**
   - Before: after a crash inside GOVERN, the remaining workflows' slot work at that minute was lost.
   - Re-running the whole step was also wrong: DETECT would see the probe GOVERN had just applied and start its test a step early.
   - Fix: the `loop_stage` markers above.
3. **False `NO_SAFE_WINDOW` pages on cap-1 workflows.**
   - Seen on variant (c) (cap 1): after a rejection, the 48-hour routine rule left no slot, and the release train paged NO_SAFE_WINDOW.
   - Fix: `no_submit_reason` tells a fatigue wait from a missing window; a fatigue wait is recorded, not paged. Unit test added, and the (c) run is now clean.
4. **Every request claimed `affected_leads: 0`.** The builder had no cells or lead counts. It now counts real leads (for example 3698 for a nw_renewal caller-ID change).

### Deviations
- **The probe learner is the default** (`CTL_LEARNER=probe`). The T1 platform tests and the run.sh test now set `none`. `CTL_LEARNER` is passed through compose and is part of `settings_hash`.
- **Decision ids are excluded from crash comparisons.** Reconciliation writes its own decision rows by design. Each side effect still has a decision row written before its call. The release train's per-workflow summary row is written when that workflow finishes, so a crash can lose that summary for the workflow in progress, but not its side effect's row.
- **Held patches auto-release with the check always passing.** The release train re-checks everything when it next composes.
- **The probe learner proposes only at routine slot minutes.**

### Open problems for the next step
1. **One harmful change on public_02.** ks_cancel v6 is a caller-ID probe onto a DID that T4's model re-admitted after it had been flagged, graded didΔ −0.021. The probe learner has no caller-ID harm gate (T3's `did_gate` with `did_posterior` is not wired). M4 must gate probes, re-admissions especially.
2. **"At least one approval applied per day where policy allows" holds only on days with work.** The probe learner has no daily work, so on most days nothing is submitted. On days it did, governance never blocked or delayed it. T2's harness J2 test, with patches forced daily, still covers the literal daily case.
3. **Verification and harm re-checks are not wired.** No kind verifier exists, so applied proposals stay VERIFYING (this blocks nothing). The harm-gate re-check after a late decision or a notice is unset and always passes. The builder's dry-run and impact replay (engagement and DID posteriors) are not wired either.
4. **Possible bug in T4's re-admission rule.** On nw_kyc, D1 was re-admitted and judged unhealthy after 57 dials (estimated pickup ratio 0.70). T4 should check its comparator.
5. **public_02 hallucinated calls rose** (471 vs 348), because more calls connect while the unsafe P2 prompt is live. Incident response is M6. ALL engagement on public_02 is −1.6%.
6. **Cost at scale is unprofiled.** `load_snapshot` runs per workflow every step in DETECT. That is fine at dev scale but untested at public_03 scale.
7. **The suite now takes about 14 minutes** (was about 6): the M3 live runs add about 4.5 minutes and the governance crash cells about 3.
8. **Every stage hook must be idempotent at a given minute.** ADR 0029 records this as a rule for later tracks.
9. **Containers:** `formi_int_pg` (55470) and `formi_int-db-1` (55471) are still running. I dropped the throwaway databases my runs left in `formi_int_pg`. `formi_t1-db-1` is untouched.
