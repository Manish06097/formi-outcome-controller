## Why I2 had a harmful change on public_02
A caller-ID probe went onto a DID the model re-admitted after 3 days although a fast-path drop had degraded it. Load cannot explain a fast-path drop, so re-probing only re-measures a bad DID on real calls. The model now re-admits only slow-path or history degradations, and every caller-ID change passes `did_posterior` then `did_gate`. public_02 now: 0 harmful (8 neutral, 6 beneficial).
