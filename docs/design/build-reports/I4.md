## Other defects found and fixed by the end-to-end runs
- Replay mismatches (see commits above): 1 ulp float differences between a live config and one read
  back from JSONB; and the stored harm-stop and rollback outcome was the filtered list.
- **Non-determinism between identical runs** (found by the governance crash matrix, which compares a
  crashed run with a clean one). Sets of strings were iterated in the engagement paths and Python
  randomises string hashing per process, so float sums ran in a different order and near-threshold
  decisions flipped. Cohort and option iteration is now sorted and allocation dicts are stored sorted.
  After the fix the 12 `probe` crash cells pass (crash equivalence with the engagement learner on).
- Crash matrix: the original matrix (3 of each intent) now runs on `CTL_LEARNER=probe_only`, and the
  full learner is crashed at the first occurrence of each intent kind and phase (47 cells, 30 governance).
- m3 live tests relaxed in two places the engagement learner legitimately changes: a supervisor edit
  lists every dim whose stored weights differ (rounded shares re-normalised), and a later gated
  allocation on top of an edit is not a revert. A change that only touches the default cohort when
  every cohort overrides that dimension truthfully reports 0 affected leads (open problem 4).
- `test_t3_stats`: single stratum variance is floored (ADR 0031), tolerance 0.5%.

Full suite (excluding `test_run_sh.py`, which needs Docker): first run 788 passed, 6 failed, 18 errors;

## 17.2 model comparison
Raw table: `docs/measurements/engagement-model-selection.md` (13 scenarios x 2 models, 0 harmful engagement
changes for both). Beta-binomial: mean gap +6.0%, 5 of 26 workflows at or below 0. Ridge-logit: +2.8%,
6 of 26. Ridge does not win the ADR 0003 rule. Default left as Beta-binomial.

## Decisions for the lead
1. Default model. Recommendation: keep Beta-binomial (higher gap in 9 of 13 scenarios; same harm). Ridge
   is flatter near zero, which is why its nw_renewal losses in p02 scenarios are smaller (-1% against
   -14%). Alternative: ridge as default for lower variance; costs about half the gain.
2. nw_renewal negative gap in every p02-family scenario (public_02, seed_p02_s2201/2202) and effects_flipped_p01. Hypothesis, not verified and not tuned
   against: public_02 has a supervisor edit that resets the default voice mid-experiment; our cohort
   overrides keep their probing shares while the baseline's default moves wholly. If true, the fix is a
   design decision: treat a supervisor edit of the default as new evidence and re-open the cohort
   allocations (or fold the default's change into the overrides). Recommendation: investigate in I-next
   before changing anything.
3. Decay at dev volume. 5.7 itself states the residual risk (1.5 to 2 days of exposure plus apply
   latency). The measured alarm is at about 4 days after onset in the rerun, and the share of the
   variant rises from 15% to 28% between days 3 and 6 while the decay develops. Options: (a) accept the
   documented risk and relax the acceptance to the design's own bar; (b) cap a new arm's share at the
   monitoring floor until its first CUSUM segments are clean (a design change to 5.4, no threshold
   change); (c) lower `h` (a threshold meaning change). Recommendation: (b).
4. `Held` caller-ID line enforced only on unproven DIDs (ADR 0031); I3's DID gate owns the rest.
