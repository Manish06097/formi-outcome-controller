## Decisions for the lead
1. Prompt fixes labelled "harmful" by the grader. In the P4-like worlds (1 per seed, 21 over 20 seeds plus unsafe-default's 2) the ranked safe fix engages 3 to 5 percent worse than the supervisor's prompt, and the grader calls the change harmful although 7.1 exempts P0 prompt fixes from the engagement gate. Every harmful change in those worlds is a `prompt_version` fix; no non-prompt change is harmful. Alternatives: (a) keep (safety first, MTTR finite, matches 7.1); (b) prefer, among SAFE candidates, the best-engaging one more strongly in the rank; (c) add an engagement tie-break only when two candidates are both FULL-SAFE. Recommend (a) now and (b) if the lead wants the harmful count at 0 on every variant.
2. Migrations 0021-0024 unused. Recommend leaving the range free.
3. A lost suggest-fixes call is not repeated (candidates are optional; the catalog rank covers it). Alternative: repeat once like validation. Recommend keep.
