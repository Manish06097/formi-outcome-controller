### 2. What I built
- **`stats/sequential.py` (5.9, 7.1, 7.5; listed as T4's in the M0 skeleton)**:
  - `BernoulliRatioSprt`, `BernoulliCusum` and `PageCusum`, each a frozen value with `to_json`/`from_json` so it survives a restart.
  - Closed-form checks: Wald's E[N] and operating characteristic, `siegmund_arl`, `flag_cusum_h`.
- **`cohorts/cells.py`, `grid.py`, `cohortset.py` (5.2)**:
  - Business cohorts stay first, verbatim and supervisor-owned.
  - F1 is picked by a deviance test with p < 0.001; F2 is `lang` or the most balanced remaining feature.
  - Cells under the 5% floor merge with the closest hour curve, then go to `other` buckets, and the result is capped at `20 - n_business`.
  - `cohort_key` is the versioned id, and `adopt()` turns a supervisor's cohort edit into a new set version.
  - `GridCohortSet` provides `membership(version)` and `winner_scope_eligible` (15% of connected calls plus a projected n = 800 look before the freeze).
- **`learning/schedule.py` (5.8)**:
  - `HourCurvesModel.fit`: curves shrunk with a 200-dial prior over a fixed attempt mix.
  - `plan_hour_weights`: kappa 2, clipped to [0.25, 4], so no in-window hour is ever 0. It refreshes only when the table moves by more than 0.1 L1, skips business tables and `default`, and waits for 3 dialling days when there is no history.
  - `verify_schedule`: the credible-band check, as a pure function.
- **`learning/did.py` (5.9, 7.3 drains)**:
  - Mantel-Haenszel ratio with the Greenland-Robins variance. A DID with zero connects uses the CMH score z instead.
  - Probes sized from the DID gate and volume, at most 2 at a time, and none during the freeze or inside an incident drain.
  - SPRT on tick batches with a concurrent comparator, truncated at 1,000 dials.
  - Slow path (UCB95 < 0.85 over 12, 24 or 48 dialling hours), re-admission after 3 days, and an equal split over healthy DIDs.
  - The no-healthy-DID rule, and P0b drain chains (`drain_step`, `drain_chain`) that only ever take share off the drained DID.
  - Also: `flag_day_share`, and `did_posterior` for T3's DID gate.
- **`incidents/detectors.py` (7.1, 7.3, 7.5)**:
  - `DidDetector`: windows of 1 h (suspect) and 2, 4, 8 h (alarm).
  - `FlagCusum`: hourly batches, plus "open on live evidence".
  - `PromptTrigger`: supervisor prompt edits and the D0 screen plan, with the cross-workflow escalation and LOW mode.
  - `DriftDetector`: observed over expected per dialling hour, adjusted for hour, attempt, cell mix and the DID factor. The downward arm alarms; the upward arm is view-only.
  - `Detectors.advance` and `detect`.
- **`incidents/safety.py` (7.1)**:
  - `SafetyVerdictRunner`: sequential, full and stage1_only modes, cached per prompt, and a CUSUM re-check pools every earlier run.
  - Gate evidence: `gate_plan` and `p_pass`. Exactly one evidence run per (decision, prompt), designated before it runs and never re-run.
  - Fix ranking: `last_known_good` and `rank_candidates`. Suggestions only add candidates; they never reorder.
- **`incidents/manager.py` (7.1, 7.3, 17.1)**:
  - Prompt incident states: open, fixing, verifying, resolved, or blocked with `NO_SAFE_PROMPT` / `CLIENT_GATE_UNREACHABLE`.
  - Exactly one suggest-fixes call per incident; the skip is recorded as a decision when credits are LOW or the call is disabled.
  - One P0a patch carrying the designated evidence run and a summary in plain words.
  - DID drain: one chain step per stop, then verification over the first 2 dialling hours.
  - KPI drift response, and `predicted_mttr`.
- **`incidents/credits.py` (7.2)**:
  - `plan_pools`, and `OrderedCreditBudget` with `authorize`, `authorize_incident` (R, then U), `top_up_reserve`, `release_screens`, `on_402` and `sync` with the API's balance.
  - `record` writes `credit_ledger` and `credit_envelope` rows.
- **Test support**: `tests/support/synth.py` (synthetic snapshots and simulators) and `tests/support/fake_sim.py` (a SimClient-shaped fake for validation and suggest-fixes).

### 4. Contract changes requested
- `settings.Thresholds`: add `winner_scope_ramp: tuple[float, ...] = (0.038, 0.12, 0.25, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50)` (section 5.5). For now it is the module constant `cohortset.WINNER_SCOPE_RAMP`.
- `FlagMonitorState` and `flag_monitor`: add `last_sim_hour int`. I am currently storing the last processed sim hour in `last_event_id`, because `r_flags` is hourly.
- `PatchRequest.decision_id` is required, but pure modules cannot write decisions. They emit `decision_id=0`, and the caller sets it with `dataclasses.replace` after recording the decision. Either make it optional or document that convention.
- `WorkflowSnapshot` has no config events and no `end_minute`. So the prompt trigger takes `ConfigChange`s directly, and the freeze uses `end_minute` passed to the constructor. Adding `end_minute` to the snapshot would simplify this.

### 5. Deviations from the design
- **Fix verification is sequential** (ADR 0009). At 300 calls a prompt that is exactly at the baseline flag rate has UCB95 above 2 p0 about half the time. A one-shot check would therefore reopen good fixes. Instead the check passes when both bounds clear, fails as soon as a bound shows the fix is bad, and otherwise waits until the 24 h window ends.
- **The worst incident W includes the 3-credit suggest-fixes call** when `CTL_LLM_SUGGEST` is on (section 17). That makes W = 68, so R = 136 at C0 = 200. The 7.2 table is still available with `llm_suggest=False`.
- **5.9 table, r = 1.6 row**: the design's E[N] of 59 dials is an arithmetic slip; the correct drift is 0.090, not 0.077, which gives about 51 dials (simulated 52).
- **"F1 neighbour" means the F1 level with the closest hour curve**, because feature levels have no natural order (ADR 0022).
- **Merging buckets**: a cohort's hour curve is shrunk at the cohort level, which is what 5.2's "200 dials per cohort-hour" implies.
- **Re-admission after 3 days covers unhealthy DIDs as well as degraded ones.**
- **suggest-fixes is called when the incident opens**, even if a last-known-good prompt exists.
- **CMH score z for zero-connect DIDs.**
- **Mantel-Haenszel lives in `learning/did.py`**, because `stats/ratios.py` belongs to T3.

### 6. Known gaps and risks
- Nothing is wired into the orchestrator or governance yet. Persisting `did_state`, `incident`, `prompt_verdict` and `validation_run` rows is the caller's job: the modules expose `rows()`, `run_log`, `decisions`, `alerts` and `did_overrides` for it.
- Validation calls must be wrapped in write-ahead intents by T1's client wrapper.
- Without T3's effect model (`engage_expect` / `engagement_of` hooks), engagement drift and live-engagement ranking fall back to raw `r_flags` rates.
- Gaps I left out:
  - schedule verification does not apply the "DID-adjusted workflow pickup" bound;
  - the drift alarm's mix-versus-within decomposition (5.10) is not computed;
  - the probe SPRT recomputes masks per tick, which is fine at dev volume but not profiled at public_03 scale.
- The flag CUSUM's reflecting barrier is applied once per hour, so the statistic can differ slightly from a per-call CUSUM.
